## ClaimTrail – Formative Project Presentation

This presentation summarises the development of my graduation project, ClaimTrail. It outlines the initial problem identification and research direction, the development of the project concept, audience research, methodological approach and initial prototyping.

It also introduces the proposed technical architecture, including multilingual information retrieval and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), alongside the planned development, testing and evaluation stages.

The purpose of this presentation is to communicate my progress so far, reflect on the decisions made during the research and design process, and identify the key challenges and areas requiring further development before the final project.
